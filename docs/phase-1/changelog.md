# Phase 1 changelog

Status: Active

This log records owner decisions and significant plan changes for Phase 1. Research implications and remaining questions are in the [open-decision inventory](../research/p1-m1-open-decisions.md).

## Entries

- 2026-11-01 — Owner interview — prefer an Apple on-device AI summary of the whole diary body for the read-only title when available; use an ellipsized first-sentence fallback if the model is unavailable or returns an empty or failed result, and save immediately. Both title paths have a strict maximum of 30 displayed characters, counting any ellipsis. Regenerate the title on body save; see [product behavior](../product-behavior.md) and [open decisions](../research/p1-m1-open-decisions.md).

Transcription initializes the body, user edits replace it, and the finished asset is a Markdown/audio pair. - 2026-11-01 — Owner architecture review — process the oldest ready recording even when an older one waits for a model or method choice; continue local transcription and diary creation while the destination is temporarily unavailable; allow confirmed removal of pending or failed recordings and their private audio. - 2026-11-01 — Architecture review — clarified local launch reconciliation, durable attempt identity, iCloud bundle validation, edit recovery, active-folder paging, and a proposed per-job completion/removal barrier in the [proposal](../architecture.md).
