# P1-M18: Optional context and pending-work reminders overview

Status: Planned

Add optional recording-start location and weather to new Diary Snippets, and one local reminder when diary work remains pending after app departure. Keep capture, transcription, saving, and in-app recovery usable when permissions, lookups, notification delivery, or platform services are unavailable.

## Entry and locked inputs

- Implementation follows P1-M17 under [Phase 1 order](phase-1.md), unless the owner authorizes a reorder. M16 has an implemented handoff with open acceptance evidence; M21 is active ahead of M17. M18 remains planning only and does not close inherited gates.
- Follow the approved [product behavior](../product-behavior.md#confirmed-v1-behavior), [P1-M5 context/reminder/privacy contracts](milestone-05-decisions-architecture.md), [network and permission boundaries](../architecture.md#network-and-local-inference), and [GUI](../ux-gui.md#planned-owner-approved-behavior). No new provider, dependency, build tool, schema, or product policy is approved by this plan.
- Consume M8's live location authorization and Settings route, M9's immutable recording identity/start time and protected capture workspace, M10's durable job ownership, M13's verified draft, and M14's publication barrier and upload/cleanup proof. Acquisition belongs to the recording, not a transcription attempt or the currently selected folder.
- Reuse M15's stored-context Metadata/Apple attribution presentation, M16's preservation of recording metadata through body/Favorite edits, and M17's authoritative pending-work and recovery handoffs. M19 retains complete-experience and adversarial acceptance.

## Implementation prerequisites

- **Actual start event:** `CaptureCoordinator` establishes `CaptureIdentity`, but composition must explicitly notify context acquisition only after audio starts successfully. A Record tap, permission sheet, or failed capture must not start a lookup. Context must never delay capture settlement while locked.
- **Durable metadata handoff:** `DiaryDraftFactory` currently supplies `location: nil, weather: nil`; capture receipts and queue jobs have no context binding. Add a confined, installation-bound context record and a verified snapshot handoff into draft creation/publication, including crash reconciliation and compatibility with older jobs.
- **Publication authority:** context sealing and opt-out must serialize with M14's exact Markdown digest/commit claim. An already prepared draft or commit record cannot authorize a later stale context write; uncertain or partly published state retains M14 recovery/proof duties.
- **Pending observation and lifecycle:** current `AppModel` arrays are presentation projections, and `becameActive()` is also called from Settings. Add a typed durable pending observation and genuine app-departure input; permission/picker interruptions and view appearance must not reset the reminder clock.
- **WeatherKit capability:** no WeatherKit entitlement is currently declared in `project.yml`. Production needs the matching app capability, account app service, and provisioning profile, plus usable attribution. Record unavailable signing/account access as a blocker; do not substitute another weather service or claim simulator results prove provisioning.

## Scope

| Work | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Location preference and permission | Retain first-launch optional When In Use permission and add the app preference to the existing Settings location section. Check preference and live authorization before acquisition, outbound lookup, result acceptance, and metadata publication. Denial, Allow Once expiry, reduced accuracy, and opt-out never block recording or trigger surprise prompts. |
| Recording-start context | Begin bounded acquisition at successful capture start. Accept valid location within the approved five-minute/500 m contract; preserve the returned coordinates without rounding or substituting geocoded coordinates. Place failure keeps coordinates. Accept weather dated within 60 minutes of recording start, store Celsius, and omit unavailable complete groups. |
| Durability and publication | Retain only validated recording-bound context in protected, backup-excluded staging. Freeze available context without waiting when drafting/publishing starts. Retry/relaunch/retargeting reuses accepted start context without reacquisition; late results cannot backfill a draft or saved asset. Serialize revocation, removal, and initial publication; clean context staging with its owning job. |
| Metadata | Reuse bottom Metadata, coordinate fallback, system measurement formatting, and Apple Weather attribution beneath displayed weather. Browsing and edits never acquire or refresh context. Preserve existing/legacy metadata and unknown or malformed optional scalar fields under the established codec/edit rules. |
| Reminder permission | Ask only when the first reminder becomes necessary and the app can present the request in the foreground. Persist the request-attempt guard, check live notification settings, and retain a nonblocking Open Settings path on denial without repeated prompts. Never present a permission sheet during active capture or from the background. |
| Reminder lifecycle | Maintain at most one generic, nonrepeating local request for eligible pending work. Anchor it to actual app departure, target the next local calendar day at the same wall time, reconcile on reopen with the current timezone and next valid DST time, and cancel when eligible work clears. Background capture settlement can update eligibility without moving the departure time. |
| Recovery and privacy | Keep Main/All pending state authoritative regardless of reminder failure/delivery. A notification opens All and rechecks current state. Its text is exactly `You have pending diary work.`; payload, logs, previews, and errors carry no diary/location/destination content. |

## Out of scope

No background location/tracking, Always or temporary precise-location request, later weather backfill, saved-metadata editor/removal, custom weather provider, hosted inference, APNs, background inference, recurring daily nag, reminder time picker, new root destination, or guarantee of notification delivery. Planning performs no account/capability change or user-data mutation.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] Typed context, preference, pending observation, calendar/clock, and reminder ports preserve inward dependencies. Platform locations/weather/notification objects, URLs, and raw errors stay inside adapters; old settings/jobs without M18 fields remain readable without invented context.
- [ ] Successful recording start launches one bounded context operation with immutable start/installation/recording identity. Failed starts launch none. Capture start, Stop & Transcribe, locked settlement, inference, title generation, and save never await a location/weather result.
- [ ] Location freshness/accuracy, finite coordinate ranges, exact returned values, current authorization/preference, and weather start-time tolerance are validated at the boundaries. Exact and just-outside thresholds, reduced accuracy, future/invalid dates, and hostile/oversized strings are covered. Geocoding failure retains coordinates; weather failure omits weather without failing the job.
- [ ] The Settings preference persists safely, preserves older settings, and cannot override system denial. Permission changes/opt-out cancel and fence lookups, clear unpublished context authority, and prevent stale results or retries from publishing it. No repeated/implicit permission requests or background location capability are introduced.
- [ ] A versioned, bounded, protected, backup-excluded context record survives capture-to-queue adoption, draft construction, retries, and crashes. Wrong-install/unbound/malformed records are isolated; restored work never triggers context lookup or publication before its owner's review. Missing optional context remains omission, not a failed recording.
- [ ] Snapshot sealing, private-draft binding, and M14 publication serialize with revocation/removal. Draft/commit digests cannot disagree; an early save proceeds with available groups and rejects later enrichment. Partial publication, protected-data failure, changed destination, and late callbacks preserve recovery copies and existing upload/cleanup guarantees.
- [ ] Stored accepted context keeps its start instant through delayed processing, current-Settings transcription Retry, explicit destination replacement, body/Favorite edits, and sync. No reopen, detail visit, model readiness change, or resumed upload reacquires or backfills context. Verified completion/confirmed removal cleans only positively owned context staging.
- [ ] WeatherKit capability/app-service/profile and real lookups work on the target phone. Weather temperature uses system measurement formatting; coordinate fallback and Apple attribution behave correctly in Metadata, including unavailable attribution, offline browsing, light/dark, and English/Korean. No unattributed weather is displayed.
- [ ] Durable pending observation deduplicates adopted captures/jobs/accepted edits, includes prerequisite/save/upload/review/removal waits across original folders, and distinguishes unknown storage from verified empty. Failed-only or external catalog recovery does not create a pending reminder; retry/Resume changes eligibility through the owning state machine.
- [ ] Notification authorization is requested once in context when first needed, with a persisted attempt guard and no capture/background prompt. Denied/disabled/changed authorization, Allow Once location sheets, installation restore, add/cancel failure, and permission-result races preserve in-app recovery without repeat prompts or false scheduling success.
- [ ] One nonrepeating generic request uses real background departure and next-local-day calendar arithmetic, current-timezone reopen reconciliation, next-valid DST handling, and a verified trigger date. Duplicate lifecycle callbacks, Settings appearance, cross-midnight capture settlement, rapid reopen/departure, overdue requests, clock/calendar changes, and stale add/cancel completions cannot duplicate or resurrect an obsolete reminder.
- [ ] Completion/removal of the last eligible item cancels pending and stale delivered reminders as soon as observed. Unknown/inaccessible state is not treated as empty; old notification taps revalidate and open current All state without restarting work or exposing stale content. Foreground delivery does not compete with the existing in-app notice.
- [ ] Focused policy, adapter, storage/fault, lifecycle, calendar, race, and privacy checks cover the [approach](milestone-18-context-reminders-architecture.md), including applicable [V-04, V-08, V-09, and V-12](../security-review.md#adversarial-verification-matrix) cases. Delegate command execution and first-pass failure analysis to `test_runner`.
- [ ] `make lint` and `TEST_DESTINATION='platform=iOS Simulator,id=<available iPhone UDID>' scripts/validate.sh` pass for the implementation; record date/revision/destination. Follow the [CoreSimulator access retry](../contribution-guide.md#coresimulator-access-in-restricted-shells) when needed; an unavailable gate stays unchecked with its blocker.
- [ ] Target-iPhone [M18-D01–D09](milestone-18-context-reminders-device-evidence.md) contain redacted passing observations for actual permission, lookup, provisioning, lock/lifecycle, scheduling/cancellation, accessibility, storage, and privacy behavior. Simulator/fake-clock evidence supplements rather than replaces those observations.
- [ ] Update affected user/UX/architecture/milestone docs and Phase 1 status with actual evidence and inherited blockers. Leave M18 incomplete while required checklist or gate evidence is missing.
