# P1-M13: Diary drafts and titles approach

Status: Active

This plan adds a method-neutral draft stage between the verified transcription candidate and M14's iCloud commit. It keeps title inference optional for progress while making candidate adoption, draft verification, and queue transitions crash safe.

## Boundaries and data flow

The application use case reads a `resultReady` claim through `TranscriptionCandidateStore.readVerified`, an audio-source identity port, a title-generation port, a draft-store port, and the queue store. It receives typed values and opaque handles; only adapters open private files or call Apple's Foundation Models APIs. The title adapter receives the current body and language, with no audio, destination, network client, tool capability, or other diary context. Keep fixed instructions separate from untrusted diary text.

The builder rechecks the persisted job immediately before and after asynchronous title work. Require the same job ID, attempt ID/generation, installation binding, source digest, method, effective settings, destination revision, body revision, and `resultReady` state. Preserve the candidate's exact text as the one `DiaryBody`; it is already bounded and usable, and M13 must not normalize it again. Convert only validated effective provenance to the approved flat fields; never serialize an arbitrary settings dictionary, an internal digest, or a placeholder as front matter. Recording-start time, explicit `en-US` or `ko-KR` hint, duration, and actual audio properties come from the verified capture receipt and audio inspection, not nominal capture settings or the title result. Write that hint to both `language` and `transcription_language_hint`; neither field is changed by the title policy's separate current-body language check.

Persist a random v4 `AssetID` in the job's durable draft identity before writing. Migrate old queue records without assigning fresh IDs to failed or unrelated work. On adoption retry or relaunch, reuse the allocated ID and the recording-start UTC layout. Freeze the initial `updated_at` with the first verified managed draft; recovery must not change it merely by rerunning title generation or preparing an upload. Use the M7 codec to write one backup-excluded, protected Markdown draft within the validated job workspace. A small versioned private binding record, separate from front matter and containing no second transcript, binds its exact bytes/digest to the job, attempt/generation, source digest, destination/body revisions, asset ID, and audio identity. The store atomically publishes and reopens each file, validates backup/protection and confinement, decodes the Markdown, and checks the binding before returning a typed verified draft handle. A job state or filename alone never proves a draft valid. M14 receives this handle plus the leased audio through separate ports, and must still verify and publish the iCloud pair.

## Title policy

Use a pure title policy around an on-device model adapter. Determine the body's current supported language with confidence at least 0.8 before requesting AI output; this may differ from the recording hint. Check output language against that body language with the same confidence threshold. When the model is available, request one faithful concise phrase under prompt revision `title-v1`, without quotes, prefix, invented facts, emoji, or ending punctuation. Treat empty, wrong-language, uncertain, structurally disallowed, or otherwise unusable output as a fallback case. Deterministic checks can reject visible format violations but cannot prove a phrase contains no invented facts; the fixed instruction and untrusted-content boundary still apply. Truncate an otherwise usable title beyond 30 Swift extended grapheme clusters to 29 plus `…` without a second request. AI origin includes prompt revision, iOS version, and exposed model identifier only when available. Never persist prompt text or intermediate summaries.

For long bodies, use token counts established by the model adapter; if an exact or safely conservative count is unavailable, fall back instead of guessing that a chunk fits. Every body part must contribute through at most eight chunks of at most 2,000 model tokens and at most two summary passes. Bound each intermediate summary to 80 graphemes and 128 output tokens, final output to 64 tokens, and the entire operation to 30 seconds including availability and language checks. If a 1 MiB body cannot be covered, any part is skipped, or the deadline/cancellation occurs, stop accepting model output and use fallback. Fence late callbacks and keep one title session at a time; an unsettled cancelled session cannot mutate a draft or cause overlapping model work, while the affected job can still take the local fallback.

The fallback trims the body, uses the native sentence tokenizer, then the first nonempty text if no sentence exists; cap the result at 30 graphemes including the ellipsis. Apply deterministic whitespace and control-character handling so a title is one displayable line without changing the managed body. Empty edited body maps to `Untitled` and `empty-body`. A saved fallback is stable until a later body save, which M16 invokes through the same policy. Model failure is represented by `first-sentence` origin, not a new error value or a blocking job state.

## Durable ordering and recovery

1. Reconcile removal tombstones and restored-work quarantine before selecting a `resultReady` job. Inspect a matching verified draft before interpreting a missing candidate; otherwise read and verify the candidate and source. Defer when protected data is locked.
2. Persist or recover the job's stable asset identity. Build the body and title without occupying the single transcription inference worker, then recheck the current claim after each suspend point. A stale result is discarded without writing.
3. Under the per-job mutation barrier, publish and reopen the draft and binding record. Treat an interrupted write of either file as uncommitted until both verify together. Recheck the claim and replace the queue job with `readyToSave` only when the verified handle matches current claim and revisions. A failed queue write leaves both verified candidate and draft for reconciliation; it pauses the queue. Destination availability is checked by M14 after this transition, not as a precondition for local draft creation.
4. After the durable transition, discard only the candidate whose closed attempt identity matches the adopted draft. Extend the current candidate-store discard contract, which only checks identity while the job is `resultReady`, so cleanup stays identity-checked after the state changes. A failed discard leaves a recoverable intermediate for later confined cleanup and must not erase the verified draft.

On relaunch, a matching verified draft wins over its still-present candidate and repairs an interrupted transition without rerunning transcription or retitling. A candidate without a verified draft is adopted normally. A draft with a mismatched claim, source, or revision cannot authorize a transition; preserve it for safe recovery or confined removal as appropriate. A missing/invalid draft while the candidate remains valid may be rebuilt with the same asset ID. If neither is verifiable, retain audio and show a typed recovery reason instead of inventing success. Update M10's current reconciliation, which fails `resultReady` when its candidate is missing, to inspect the draft first; `readyToSave` must validate the draft without requiring the discarded candidate. Confirmed removal fences all title and storage callbacks and waits for draft/candidate access to relinquish before cleanup. M14 alone advances a draft to saved and deletes private staging after pair verification and both upload reports.

## Verification sequence

1. Add the draft identity/state and ports without changing M10's transcription scheduling. Extend queue migration, removal cleanup, and reconciliation with focused old-record and discarded-candidate fixtures.
2. Implement the pure title policy, fake model adapter, language checks, fallback, and bounded long-body coverage. Test Unicode and adversarial diary text before connecting the platform adapter.
3. Add the protected draft store through M7's codec/workspace and fault injection at each draft/binding write, reopen, state replace, and intermediate cleanup. Test Apple and Cohere candidate fixtures with real provenance validation, including substitution of same-size Markdown and audio bytes.
4. Connect the on-device title adapter and exercise cancellation, 30-second timeout, lifecycle changes, and no-network/privacy boundaries. Run affected tests while iterating, then the full lint and simulator gate once.
5. Run M13-D01–M13-D05 on the target iPhone and record only redacted environment, state, timing, and pass/fail observations in the [device evidence record](milestone-13-diary-drafts-titles-device-evidence.md).

## Implemented handoff and current evidence

`DiaryDraftAdoptionCoordinator` selects a current `resultReady` job, reserves its asset ID in the queue, reads the verified candidate, generates a bounded title, and rechecks the entire job after asynchronous work. `DiaryDraftFactory` maps only the verified recording receipt and effective method provenance into a v1 `DiarySnippetV1`; `FileDiaryDraftStore` publishes the Markdown and a private attempt/source/draft-digest binding record, then reopens both and verifies the audio source before the queue enters `readyToSave`. `RetryingDiaryDraftStore` defers access during a locked launch and retries after protected data is available. Queue reconciliation accepts a matching verified draft before requiring a candidate, and candidate discard remains attempt-checked after the transition. M14 still owns iCloud publication and final private staging deletion.

The `title-v1` policy uses the local `SystemLanguageModel` with native language recognition, exact model token counts, bounded chunks and output, and a 30-second fallback deadline. Its late model task cannot change a returned title or start overlapping title work before settlement. Simulator tests cover policy limits, record migration, both method paths, restoration quarantine, protected-data and queue-store deferral, asset-ID and write/read faults, source and draft/binding substitution, post-transition cleanup, and removal during title work. `make lint` and the full `scripts/validate.sh` gate passed on the booted iPhone 18 Pro simulator on 2026-11-02. The [target-iPhone matrix](milestone-13-diary-drafts-titles-device-evidence.md) remains unrun because no physical iPhone was discoverable; production file-protection, model-availability, lifecycle, and privacy claims still need that evidence.
