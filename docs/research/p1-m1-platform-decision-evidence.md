# P1-M1: Apple platform decision evidence

Status: Active

This research narrows unresolved iCloud, Apple speech, title, privacy, reminder, and recording-context decisions without approving implementation choices. It relies on Apple platform documentation checked on 2026-11-01; behavior in an arbitrary user-selected iCloud Drive folder and on the target iPhone still needs a device prototype. Later owner answers are recorded in [product behavior](../product-behavior.md) and the [Phase 1 changelog](../phase-1/changelog.md); recommendations below are research history, not current decisions.

## iCloud destination and commit

Apple's [directory picker guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/providing-access-to-directories) says a folder picker may return a directory from local storage, iCloud, or a third-party File Provider. A picked directory has a security-scoped URL that permits recursive access and can be bookmarked. For each operation, the app must start security-scoped access, coordinate reads or writes, then stop access. Apple explicitly warns that bookmark resolution, scope acquisition, and later I/O can fail after the user changes permission. Its [document picker reference](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uidocumentpickerviewcontroller) requires file coordination for external documents and says to persist a bookmark rather than the returned URL.

**Inference:** The folder picker alone cannot establish the v1 requirement that a selected destination is iCloud Drive. The implementation needs a verified way to identify and reject local or third-party selections, plus a target-device trial of creating a folder in Files and later reopening its bookmark. Neither the picker documentation nor a successful bookmark resolution proves permanent access. Distinguish a temporarily unavailable provider, a revoked scope, a deleted folder, and an iCloud account or quota problem from actual observations; do not retarget a pending job on an arbitrary timeout.

Apple's [iCloud document sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/synchronizing-documents-in-the-icloud-environment) coordinates access and resolves *a document's* versions using `NSFileVersion.modificationDate`. That date is [optional for a deleted version](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/nsfileversion/modificationdate). [NSFileVersion conflict guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/nsfileversion/isconflict) describes one current version and other conflicting versions of an individual file. [Resolution guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/nsfileversion/isresolved) says obsolete versions must be explicitly removed after resolution or they continue to sync and consume quota. **Inference:** Markdown/audio is an application-level pair, not a single `NSFileVersion` unit. A timestamp winner for Markdown does not prove that the referenced audio exists, matches, or has uploaded. Before deleting a local repair candidate, verify the referenced audio and parsed Markdown together under coordinated access, with matching asset identity and expected bytes or digests. The owner-approved latest-modification-time rule still governs comparable conflict versions; equal or missing dates still require a user choice.

For files managed as ubiquitous items, [`ubiquitousItemIsUploadedKey`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/urlresourcekey/ubiquitousitemisuploadedkey) reports whether an individual item uploaded and recommends asynchronous `NSMetadataQuery` or `NSFilePresenter` observation. [`FileManager.isUbiquitousItem(at:)`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/filemanager/isubiquitousitem%28at%3A%29) only indicates that an item is targeted for iCloud; it does not establish upload. Apple lists [quota and server-unavailable errors](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/icloud-error-codes). **Open:** Test whether these signals apply reliably to files in a directory picked from iCloud Drive through Files. Define whether “complete iCloud pair verified” means coordinated local readability of both items or confirmed remote upload of both items. The latter may delay private staging cleanup indefinitely when offline or over quota. Neither signal by itself proves that a second device has received the pair.

### Minimum physical-device trial

Use the same Apple ID on two devices, with the target iPhone writing to a user-created iCloud Drive folder. Record the provider identity and bookmark behavior after relaunch, Files permission revocation/restoration, folder deletion/recreation, account sign-out, airplane mode, and quota failure if a controlled account permits it. Interrupt each Markdown/audio write and reopen; verify the incomplete-pair recovery rule and whether local or upload verification allows staging deletion. Edit the Markdown on both devices while offline, then reconnect and inspect current/conflict version dates and audio references. Repeat with equal or missing dates through a test seam. The trial needs a second device and controlled test data; documentation cannot settle these observations.

## Apple transcription readiness

[`SpeechTranscriber`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/speechtranscriber) exposes device availability and supported versus installed locales. [`AssetInventory`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/assetinventory) installs system-managed speech assets; it may reserve a locale, download assets, and later unsubscribe an unused reservation. An [installation request](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/assetinventory/assetinstallationrequest%28supporting%3A%29) may be absent when assets are already installed and may throw when the module is unsupported or reservations are exhausted. These facts support a Settings preflight state machine: unsupported device/locale, supported but not installed, downloading, installed and ready, and transient failure. **Inference:** Persisting a one-time “ready” boolean would be stale because system assets and permissions can change; refresh readiness in Settings and before claiming a queued job. A recording must remain waiting with an entry-level reason when its selected method is not ready. On the target phone, verify `en-US` and `ko-KR` independently with airplane mode after installation and with the selected method disappearing or failing between preflight and inference. Published API support is not a quality or five-minute performance measurement.

## Title counting and long bodies

Swift [`Character`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swift/character/) represents an extended grapheme cluster, the standard approximation of a visible character; UTF-8 bytes and Unicode scalar counts differ for emoji and composed text. This gives a concrete candidate for the owner's 30-displayed-character limit: count Swift `Character` elements after trimming and allocate space for the ellipsis inside the 30. It needs owner approval because screen layout can still render a 30-grapheme title on more than one line. Test Hangul syllables and decomposed Jamo, combining marks, emoji sequences, flags, and newline/control output.

Apple's [Foundation Models guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/generating-content-and-performing-tasks-with-foundation-models) says the default on-device model has a 4,096-token context window and recommends separate sessions for chunks of a long input. Its [context-window guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/managing-the-context-window) counts instructions and outputs as well as body text; Korean can consume roughly one token per character, so a fixed Swift-character chunk size is unsafe. It recommends `tokenCount(for:)` and a bounded combine pass. The [language guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundationmodels/supporting-languages-and-locales-with-foundation-models) provides supported languages and an unsupported-language error. **Research recommendation:** Treat chunking as an iterative, capped reduction with fresh sessions, token-budgeted input/output, and a deterministic fallback if the full body cannot fit within the allowed passes or a result is unusable. This preserves the owner's “whole body” intent without an unbounded recursive call chain. The exact chunk overlap, cap, language check, and prompt revision remain implementation decisions; any clipping that drops diary text needs explicit review.

## Recording-time location and weather

Core Location reports a timestamp and [`horizontalAccuracy`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/corelocation/cllocation/horizontalaccuracy) as a radius in meters; a negative value means invalid coordinates. [`accuracyAuthorization`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/corelocation/cllocationmanager/accuracyauthorization) may be reduced, in which case setting a more precise desired accuracy has no effect. Apple's [location guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/corelocation/getting-the-current-location-of-a-device) recommends the lowest accuracy needed and stopping updates when no longer needed. A [reverse-geocode request](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/corelocation/clgeocoder/reversegeocodelocation%28_%3Apreferredlocale%3Acompletionhandler%3A%29) is asynchronous, server-backed, and rate limited. **Inference:** Exact stored coordinates should mean the coordinates returned by Core Location, not a promise of precise positioning. A place name can fail independently of coordinates; the current product text should specify whether coordinates alone are retained when reverse geocoding fails.

WeatherKit [`CurrentWeather.date`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/weatherkit/currentweather/date) describes the weather observation time, which can differ from the request or recording time. [`WeatherService`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/weatherkit/weatherservice) provides current and date-range datasets, and supplies required attribution. **Research recommendation:** Anchor optional context to the recording-start instant, keep the location and weather observation timestamps internally until validation, and accept weather only if its observation time falls within an approved tolerance of the start instant. This is a proposal, not a product decision: the owner still needs to choose a location age/accuracy threshold and whether “recording time” means start, midpoint, or stop. A late result must respect an intervening permission or Settings change, and a failed lookup must not delay diary creation or trigger later backfill.

## Protected staging, restore, and reminders

Apple's [file-encryption guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/encrypting-your-app-s-files) distinguishes Complete Protection (unavailable while locked) from Complete Unless Open (an already-open file can remain usable after lock). The [Complete Unless Open reference](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/urlfileprotection/completeunlessopen) also says a new file can be created while locked. This narrows the capture choice: if recording can continue through lock, test an open audio handle, finalization, and queue-journal replacement separately; the audio's protection class alone does not make journal writes safe. If capture ends on lock, Complete Protection for closed staging is simpler, but the owner must approve that user-visible behavior. A physical-device test is required because simulator behavior does not prove Data Protection behavior on lock.

Apple's [backup guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/optimizing-your-app-s-data-for-icloud-backup) says `tmp` and Caches are purgeable and should not hold the only copy of user data. `isExcludedFromBackup` is guidance to the system, not a guarantee that bytes never appear in a backup or restore. The decision is therefore a real privacy versus device-loss-recovery tradeoff for pending audio and unsaved edits. A restore from a backup made before confirmed removal can resurrect old state unless another durable authority records that removal; do not promise otherwise.

Apple says to [check notification authorization before scheduling](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/asking-permission-to-use-notifications); with [denied status](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/unnotificationsettings/authorizationstatus), the system ignores scheduling attempts. [`UNCalendarNotificationTrigger`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/uncalendarnotificationtrigger) can target calendar date and time components, and [`nextTriggerDate()`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/uncalendarnotificationtrigger/nexttriggerdate%28%29) exposes the calculated next fire date. **Research recommendation:** Schedule one nonrepeating, generic pending-work reminder for the next local calendar day, inspect the computed fire date, and recalculate on later app departures if pending work remains. Test daylight-saving transitions and time-zone changes. If permission is denied, keep in-app pending state and offer a Settings recovery explanation; the owner still needs to decide whether and when to suppress a reminder after work completes while the app is closed.

## Owner decisions and evidence still required

1. The owner requires a complete locally verified pair and successful upload reports for both files before deleting staging. Verify that the upload signal works in arbitrary picked iCloud folders on two devices.
2. The owner approved Swift grapheme counting and bounded whole-body coverage with fallback. P1-M5 later fixed the exact chunk/pass limits, asset schema, and resource limits; see the [approved contracts](../phase-1/milestone-05-decisions-architecture.md).
3. The owner chose recording start as the context instant, accepts a location sample no older than five minutes with horizontal accuracy no worse than 500 m, and accepts weather observations within 60 minutes. Coordinates remain after geocoding failure.
4. The owner chose continued recording during lock and backup exclusion for pending audio and unsaved drafts. Pending jobs found after an older backup restore are quarantined for user review before processing or save. Measure protection behavior on a physical phone and define restore detection/review mechanics.
5. The owner chose reminder cancellation after all pending work finishes. Notification denial leaves in-app pending state and a nonblocking system Settings path without repeated prompts. P1-M5 later fixed the generic reminder and recovery copy. Verify Apple `en-US`/`ko-KR` readiness on the target phone.

These are decision prerequisites or later device checks for P1-M5 and the feature milestones. The owner closed P1-M1 with the documented research and assigned the Cohere target-phone comparison and runtime choice to P1-M6; see the [P1-M1 checklist](../phase-1/milestone-01-research-overview.md#completion-checklist).
